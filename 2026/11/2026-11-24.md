# 2026-11-24

## What I tried
Using Codex for writing and strategy work, not code. I organised it into projects (Haavn, my bio, a newsletter), with Notion connected so it could pull in context from my pages.

## What I learned
Projects keep each workstream's context separate, so I stop re-explaining. Connecting Notion means the AI works from my actual material instead of whatever I paste in.

## So what
"Coding agents" are general-purpose work agents. For non-technical teams, the setup (projects plus connected sources) matters more than the prompt.
