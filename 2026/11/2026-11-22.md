# 2026-11-22

## What I tried
Creating Claude skills by codifying a conversation, instead of writing the skill from a blank page.

## What I learned
The quickest way to create a skill is to codify the conversation. Do the task once with Claude, steering and correcting as you go, then ask Claude to turn that conversation into a skill. The back-and-forth already holds the steps, the edge cases and the corrections, which is most of what a good skill needs.

## So what
This lowers the bar for teams building reusable AI workflows. Nobody has to sit down and write instructions from scratch: do the work well once, then capture it.
